Terms of Service

The rules for using iTULOD as a customer or a rider.

iTULOD is a capstone project built for academic purposes. It is not yet a registered business, and no real money is charged — GCash payments run in PayMongo's test mode.

By creating an account or using the iTULOD website or Android app ("iTULOD", "we"), you agree to these terms. If you don't agree, please don't use iTULOD.

1. What iTULOD does

iTULOD connects customers with independent riders in Ilocos Norte for rides, food delivery, parcel delivery and hauling. Riders are not employees of iTULOD; they choose which jobs to accept. Service is limited to Ilocos Norte — pickups and drop-offs outside the province can't be booked.

2. Your account

  • Give your real name and a working mobile number and email, and keep them up to date.
  • Keep your password private. You're responsible for what happens on your account.
  • One account per person. You must be at least 18 years old to sign up as a rider.
  • We may suspend an account that breaks these terms or puts others at risk.

3. Booking as a customer

  • The fare shown before you book is an estimate based on the vehicle and distance. For food and parcel jobs, the rider confirms the final amount at pickup.
  • Be at the pickup point on time, and only send items that are legal and safe to carry.
  • You can cancel free of charge before a rider accepts. Cancelling after a rider has accepted, or not showing up, may cost a cancellation fee (shown before you confirm). The fee goes to the rider.
  • Refunds for prepaid (GCash or wallet) bookings go to your iTULOD wallet, less any fee.

4. Driving as a rider

  • You need a valid driver's license, your vehicle's OR/CR, a clear photo of your face and a photo of your vehicle. An admin reviews these before you can accept jobs.
  • You only receive jobs that match your registered vehicle.
  • Follow traffic laws, carry customers and items safely, and take a proof-of-delivery photo when you drop off food, parcels or hauled items.
  • iTULOD keeps a commission on each completed booking (currently 15%, shown in your earnings). Tips and cancellation fees go to you in full.
  • A new profile photo needs admin approval, so customers always see the person who was approved.

5. Payments

You can pay by cash, GCash or your iTULOD wallet. In this capstone version, GCash and wallet top-ups use PayMongo's test mode, so no real money is moved.

6. Things you must not do

  • Use iTULOD for anything illegal, or to harass, threaten or cheat anyone.
  • Tamper with fares, payments or bookings, or try to access other people's accounts or data.
  • Upload fake documents or photos of someone else.

7. Limits

iTULOD is provided as it is, as a student project. We do our best to keep it working and accurate, but we can't promise it will always be available or error-free, and we aren't responsible for losses caused by riders, customers or events outside our control.
