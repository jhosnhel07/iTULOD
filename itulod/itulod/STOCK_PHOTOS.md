# Sample dish photos

The menu-item and menu-banner "sample" photos in the `catalog-images` storage bucket
(`stock/<dish>.jpg`, see `sql/027_catalog_images.sql` and `sql/028_stock_menu_photos.sql`) are
**CC0 (no rights reserved) or public-domain** images found through [Openverse](https://openverse.org)
(mostly hosted on Wikimedia Commons). No permission or attribution is legally required for
these licences; the sources are listed here anyway for the record.

The apps label them **"Sample photo"** — they show the kind of dish, not a restaurant's actual
food. A restaurant's own photo uploaded in the admin panel replaces the sample for that item
(sql/028 only fills items that have no photo).

Each image was resized to 800×600 (centre-cropped to 4:3) before upload.
