iTULOD

Privacy Policy

What personal data we collect, why, who sees it, and your rights.

iTULOD is a capstone project built for academic purposes. We still treat your data with care, in the spirit of the Philippine Data Privacy Act of 2012 (Republic Act No. 10173).

1. What we collect

  • Account details: name, email, mobile number, password (stored only as a secure hash by our login provider — nobody, including us, can read it), and an optional profile photo.
  • Bookings: pickup and drop-off addresses and map pins, items or parcel details, fares, payment method and status, ratings, reviews, tips and chat messages with your rider or customer.
  • Riders only: driver's license number and photo, OR/CR, a photo of your face, a vehicle photo, plate number, optional NBI clearance, and your location while you're online or on a job.
  • Proof of delivery and receipts: photos riders take at drop-off or of a food receipt.
  • Device information needed to send you notifications, if you turn them on.

2. Why we use it

  • To create your account, match bookings with riders who have the right vehicle, show live tracking, calculate fares and process payments.
  • To review rider applications and keep customers safe.
  • To send booking updates, handle support requests and refunds, and prevent fraud.

3. Who can see it

  • Your rider sees your name, mobile number and the addresses for the booking they accepted.
  • Your customer sees the rider's name, profile photo, vehicle, plate number, rating and live location — only while the booking is active.
  • Before booking, customers only see nearby online riders as approximate dots on the map (about 100 m accuracy), never names.
  • iTULOD admins can see account, booking and rider-document details to approve riders, help with support requests and fix problems.
  • Service providers that run iTULOD for us: Supabase (database, login and file storage), PayMongo (payments, test mode), Mapbox and OpenStreetMap services (maps, addresses and routes), and Vercel (website hosting). We don't sell your data.

4. Location

Riders share their location only while they've turned Online on, or while they have an active job; turning Offline stops it. Customers' pins are used to price and route the booking.

5. How long we keep it

We keep your account and booking history while your account exists, so you can see past trips and receipts. Rider documents are kept while you're a rider. Ask us to delete your account and we'll remove your personal data, except what we must keep for records of completed payments.

6. Your rights

Under the Data Privacy Act you can:

  • be told what data we hold about you and get a copy of it;
  • correct wrong or outdated details (you can edit most of them in your Profile);
  • object to processing, or ask us to delete your data;
  • complain to the National Privacy Commission if you think your data was mishandled.

7. Keeping it safe

Data is sent over encrypted connections. Database rules make sure each person can only read and change what's theirs; rider documents are stored privately and only admins can open them.

8. Contact

For any privacy request, email support@itulod.app. See also our Terms of Service.
